Key UX Details

  • Card animation: New steps slide up from bottom, previous steps collapse into a thin bar showing their summary (e.g. "John Smith", "(555) 123-4567")
  • Back navigation: Tap a collapsed card or the Back button to expand it and edit
  • Company branding: Logo + primary color applied to progress dots, buttons, and active card accents
  • Configurable steps: License photo and NDA signing can be toggled on/off per company in admin settings
  • Auto-reset: Confirmation screen returns to Welcome after 15 seconds of inactivity
  • Reason presets: Common reasons as tap-to-select buttons (Meeting, Delivery, Interview, Contractor, Other)
